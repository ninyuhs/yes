ABOUT

Hobart "Hobie" Brown is Spider-Punk, an anti-establishment, anarchist Spider-Man from Earth-138 who fights corporate oppression and fascist regimes with pure punk-rock rebellion!

POWERS

Along with standard Spider-Man abilities (wall-crawling, super strength, and spider-sense), he wields an electric guitar that unleashes powerful sonic shockwaves to disarm enemies and smash brainwashing tech.
