Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse (2023) catapults Brooklyn’s teenage hero Miles Morales into the Multiverse, where he reunites with Gwen Stacy and encounters the Spider-Society—a coalition of Spider-Heroes dedicated to protecting existence.

When a tragic conflict over fixed "canon events" forces Miles to choose between saving someone he loves and saving every universe, he rebels against Spider-Man 2099 to forge his own destiny.

Pushing animation boundaries with visually distinct art styles for every dimension, the film delivers a high-octane, deeply emotional story about agency, family, and what it truly takes to be a hero.
